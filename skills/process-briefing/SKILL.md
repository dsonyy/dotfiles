# Process briefing

Collect everything on the matter, then write the brief.

Sources:
- Gmail: all threads, including sent messages.
- Google Calendar: past and upcoming events.
- Notes and transcripts in `~/brain` by default. Search the working directory too.
- Web search, only for gaps such as who a person is.

Format, in the note's language:
- Headline: the nearest meeting (date, time, link).
- Sections with bullets: who they are, the people, the subject (role, offer, deal).
- **Timeline**: numbered, dated, from first contact to the next step.
- **Gaps**: what we do not know.
- **Next steps**: a checkbox list, ending with a CTA.

Save the brief as a new note next to the matter's existing notes. 
Update any tracker it appears in (e.g. `JOBS.md`).
